# Creative treatment

The selected interpretation follows a quiet coastal railway, a woman travelling through the present, warm memories of the two characters, a final night and a calm morning. The recurring visual objects are the camera, empty seats and places that retain a person after they leave.

The storyboard is intentionally kept separate from the measurements in `audio/`. The opening title is the confirmed song title `ONE LAST KISS`; it is not replaced by a translated or invented work title.
